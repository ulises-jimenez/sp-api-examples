Taxation complexities
    1. Moving inventory from one country to another creates placeholder transactions that you do not have to pay VAT tax on. This trips up a lot of companies
    2. Inventory transfers have to be declared locally (by country) to each tax authority using the sellers VAT number
    3. Output VAT is the VAT you charge to your customers
    4. Input VAT is the VAT you pay to suppliers
    5. Net VAT owed is output VAT minus input VAT
    6. Amazon fees (FBA, referral, etc) are added to input VAT
    7. FBA removals/disposals are added to output VAT (this differs by country perhaps, need to double check)
    8. Reimbursements (payments to sellers for amz errors) from amazon also affect VAT numbers. This could possibly be different in each jurisdiction
    9. Discounts/Promotions also effect output VAT
    10. Local sales (sale takes place in the same country as inventory) are taxed and paid directly where the sale took place
    11. Cross border sales are recorded into OSS and paid into "home jurisdiction country" For BBG this would be Germany
        note: these sales need to be tallied by destination country, and have VAT tax summed up. VAT reductions also need to be applied
    12. Gift wrapping and shipping are taxed differently in different countries
    13. Every country has different VAT rates
